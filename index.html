OUR JOURNEY

THE HISTORY OF TEAM OCTAESPORTS

Our Journey. Our Ambition. Our Legacy.

Founded on 1 January 2026, Team OctaEsports began its journey with a vision to build a competitive esports organization driven by passion, teamwork, discipline, and continuous improvement. Built around the world of Battlegrounds Mobile India (BGMI), Octa represents a new generation of players determined to develop their skills, compete against strong opponents, and create their own identity in India's growing esports scene.

From the beginning, Team OctaEsports has focused on more than just playing the game. The organization aims to create a competitive environment where players can practice together, learn from every match, strengthen their coordination, and work towards bigger opportunities in esports.

JANUARY 2026

The Beginning

Every esports organization starts with a vision. With Nova and OExA1PHA (Naved Patel) serving as co-owners, the organization began developing its identity, building its roster, and working towards establishing a presence in the BGMI competitive community. The early journey has been about laying a strong foundation: bringing players together, developing team chemistry, establishing roles, and creating an environment where individual talent can contribute to collective success.

PRACTICE & RIGOR

Building the Competitive Foundation

Practice is an essential part of every competitive team's development. Team OctaEsports has participated in regular scrims with Tortuga Esports and Arise Gaming, using these opportunities to improve coordination, decision-making, combat execution, rotations, and overall team performance. For Octa, progress is built through repeated effort, teamwork, and the willingness to improve.

OFFICIAL TOURNAMENTS

Entering the Competitive Arena

  • Participated in the in-game qualifiers for KRAFTON × TRIDENT: Ignite The Spark, gaining valuable experience in a intense competitive tournament environment.
  • Played Round 1 of BGMI: NAYE KHILADI, marking another step in its ongoing competitive journey and reflecting its ambition to challenge itself against top national teams.
